🌿 Pesto Pasta

Description

Fresh basil, parmesan, and pine nuts blended into perfection.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ¼ cup pine nuts
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Black pepper (to taste)

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until al dente according to the package instructions.
  2. Reserve about ½ cup of the pasta water, then drain the pasta.
  3. In a food processor, combine the basil, Parmesan cheese, pine nuts, garlic, and olive oil.
  4. Process until smooth, adding more olive oil if needed to reach your desired consistency.
  5. Add the cooked spaghetti to the pesto and toss well to coat it in the sauce.

If needed, add a little reserved pasta water to create a silky sauce.

Season with salt and black pepper to taste.

Serve immediately with additional Parmesan cheese, if desired.

⏱️ Recipe Information

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Cooking Time: 15 minutes

Total Time: 25 minutes

Servings: 2

Difficulty: Easy

💡 Chef's Tip

To make the pesto more vibrant and flavorful, use fresh basil leaves that are bright green and aromatic. Toasting the pine nuts before adding them to the pesto can also enhance their nutty flavor.

🍽️ Best Served With

Garlic bread

Grilled vegetables (zucchini, bell peppers, or asparagus)

Caesar salad or a fresh garden salad

Garlic mushrooms

Grilled chicken or grilled shrimp (optional)

Fresh lemonade, sparkling water, or iced tea
