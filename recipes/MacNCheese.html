🧀 Mac & Cheese

Description

Mac & Cheese is the ultimate comfort food, featuring tender macaroni coated in a rich, creamy cheese sauce. This classic dish is quick, satisfying, and loved by both kids and adults. Perfect as a main course or a delicious side dish.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 200 g elbow macaroni
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp paprika (optional)
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Chopped parsley (for garnish)

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the macaroni until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat.
  3. Stir in the flour and cook for about 1 minute, whisking continuously to form a smooth paste.
  4. Gradually pour in the milk while whisking until the mixture is smooth.
  5. Simmer for 3–4 minutes until the sauce begins to thicken.
  6. Add the cheddar cheese and Parmesan cheese, stirring until completely melted and creamy.
  7. Season with salt, black pepper, and paprika if desired.
  8. Add the cooked macaroni and stir until every piece is evenly coated with the cheese sauce.
  9. Garnish with chopped parsley and serve hot.

If needed, add a little reserved pasta water to create a silky sauce.

Season with salt and black pepper to taste.

Serve immediately with additional Parmesan cheese, if desired.

⏱️ Recipe Information

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Cooking Time: 20 minutes

Total Time: 30 minutes

Servings: 2–3

Difficulty: Easy

💡 Chef's Tip

For an extra cheesy flavor, mix cheddar and mozzarella together. If you enjoy a crispy topping, transfer the mac & cheese to a baking dish, sprinkle with breadcrumbs and extra cheese, then bake for 10 minutes until golden brown.
