🍅 Arrabbiata Pasta

Description

Arrabbiata Pasta is a bold and flavorful Italian classic known for its spicy tomato sauce. Made with garlic, ripe tomatoes, olive oil, and red chili flakes, this dish delivers the perfect balance of heat and tanginess. It's a quick, delicious meal that's perfect for anyone who loves a little spice.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 200 g penne pasta (or your preferred pasta)
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1½ cups tomato puree
  • 1 tsp red chili flakes (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt (to taste)
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp fresh basil or parsley, chopped
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  • Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until al dente. Reserve ½ cup of pasta water, then drain.
  • Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  • Add the chopped garlic and sauté until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  • Stir in the red chili flakes and cook for another 30 seconds.
  • Add the tomato puree and Italian seasoning. Simmer for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  • Season the sauce with salt and black pepper.
  • Add the cooked pasta to the skillet and toss well until evenly coated.
  • If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of the reserved pasta water.
  • Garnish with fresh basil or parsley and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese, if desired.

    • ⏱️ Recipe Information

    Preparation Time: 10 minutes

    Cooking Time: 20 minutes

    Total Time: 30 minutes

    Servings: 2

    Difficulty: Easy

    💡 Chef's Tip

    For the best flavor, use fresh garlic and let the tomato sauce simmer slowly. If you enjoy extra heat, add more chili flakes or a finely chopped fresh red chili.
