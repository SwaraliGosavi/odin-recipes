🍝 Creamy Alfredo Pasta

Description

Creamy Alfredo Pasta is a rich and comforting Italian-inspired dish made with tender fettuccine coated in a silky Parmesan cream sauce. It's quick to prepare, incredibly satisfying, and perfect for a cozy family dinner or a special occasion.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 200 g fettuccine (or any pasta of your choice)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Salt (to taste)
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley (for garnish)
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning (optional)

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until al dente. Reserve ½ cup of pasta water, then drain.
  2. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add the minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Pour in the heavy cream and let it simmer gently for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Gradually stir in the grated Parmesan cheese until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.
  6. Season with black pepper, salt, and Italian seasoning if using.

Add the cooked pasta to the skillet and toss until every strand is coated in the creamy sauce.

If the sauce is too thick, stir in a little reserved pasta water until you reach your desired consistency.

Garnish with chopped parsley and extra Parmesan cheese before serving.

⏱️ Recipe Information

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Cooking Time: 15 minutes

Total Time: 25 minutes

Servings: 2

Difficulty: Easy

💡 Chef's Tip

the creamiest Alfredo sauce, use freshly grated Parmesan cheese instead of pre-shredded cheese. Fresh cheese melts more smoothly and gives the sauce a rich, velvety texture.
